                        Tasker & automation

                        Ignition broadcasts these intents (use Tasker's Intent Received event):

                        ca.heeney.ignition.COUNTDOWN
                        ca.heeney.ignition.LIFTOFF
                        ca.heeney.ignition.MECO

                        Extras: minutes, starts_at, ends_at (unix seconds), source. To ignite from Tasker, send broadcast ca.heeney.ignition.IGNITE (package ca.heeney.ignition) with extras minutes and optional delay.

                        Optional. Point this at an ignition server to share lists, burns and settings with your other devices.
